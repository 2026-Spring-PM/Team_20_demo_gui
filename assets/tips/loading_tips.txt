# Gameplay basics
Press T while standing in the village to open the bulk farm action menu.
Equip a weapon AND a magic stone -- one covers physical, the other magic damage.
Don't try to leave the village mid-wave. Henry won't ready a horse with monsters loose.
The east-west road through the village splits the farm from the workshops.

# NPCs + buildings
Tristan in the Town Hall manages village-level upgrades and per-building tiers.
Otto at the Smithy enhances weapons. Bring mana crystals and prisms.
Greta, Ros, and Karl run the three production buildings along the farm's north edge.
Markus guards the storehouse -- spillover crops go there when your bag fills up.
Charles tends the pen. Talk to him to bulk-feed or bulk-collect from the herd.

# Exploration
The City carries goods the Village vendors don't stock. Trade often.
Iola sells rare items in the City -- check her shop after you upgrade the village.
Buildings with doors can be entered. Stand at the door and press E.

# Time and weather
A whole in-game hour passes every minute of real play. Sleep advances time faster.
Weather affects crop growth. Storms damage seedlings; sunny days speed harvests.
Bruno's gambling table closes after five hands per season. Pick your battles.

# Quality of life
Q swaps between equipped weapons. Number keys 1-4 swap hotbar consumables.
Watering plots before sunrise gives a small growth bonus on most crops.
The almanac in the plaza predicts the next three days of weather.
