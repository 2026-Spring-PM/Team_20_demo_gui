tiles.png is composed from two Kenney pixel-art packs, both released
under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):

  Tiny Town     — https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town
  Tiny Dungeon  — https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon

CC0 does not require attribution, but Kenney requests a credit or
donation as a courtesy if you use the work in a public project. See
https://kenney.nl for both options.

Source tiles used (Kenney sheet, col, row):
  Grass v0-v2:  Tiny Town    (0, 0)   plain green
  Grass v3:     Tiny Town    (1, 0)   subtle grass tufts
  Path v0-v3:   Tiny Town    (1, 2)   plain sand
  Soil v0-v2:   Tiny Dungeon (0, 0)   plain dirt
  Soil v3:      Tiny Dungeon (1, 0)   dirt with debris
  Wall v0-v3:   Tiny Dungeon (1, 3)   horizontal brick

Water tiles in tiles.png are NOT from Kenney -- neither pack ships
water tiles. They are synthesised in scripts/build_atlas.py using a
flat blue (RGB 0,153,219) sampled from the blue-potion sprite in
Tiny Dungeon (8, 9), so they sit in the same colour vocabulary as
the rest of the atlas without clashing.

To regenerate the atlas after replacing tile picks:
  python3 scripts/build_atlas.py
